Who We Are

The AI Infrastructure
Company

AIGRID Ventures LLC is an AI infrastructure company focused on the investment, development, deployment and operation of next-generation AI computing facilities. We specialize in delivering scalable AI infrastructure for enterprise customers, AI startups, cloud providers, research institutions and hyperscale partners.

Our expertise spans the complete lifecycle of AI infrastructure — from site acquisition and power strategy to GPU deployment, managed hosting and 24×7 operations.
